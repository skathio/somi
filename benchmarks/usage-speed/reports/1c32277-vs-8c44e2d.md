## Limits

- Per release, not per commit: the result covers every change in the release together.
- The verdict is for the recorded model tier only; context-size effects transfer in direction, sizing and
  other behaviour effects do not.
- L0 is exact bytes injected, not tokens billed. L1 is one host fingerprint. Live L2 resolves only large
  changes (about 50% or more); smaller changes read inconclusive by design.
- Quality is a coarse non-inferiority flag only; no quality win or loss is declared.
- A first baseline recording on 2026-10-25 was discarded: the A/B driver did not recognise ship-loop's
  Stage -1 gate when the model laid it out as a list, so 9 of 16 S2/S3 SoMi draws ended at the gate unresumed.
  Fixed in 1c32277 before both arms here were recorded.
- On S2 (T1-proration) the SoMi pipeline often stops at its plan-loop pass cap or on the spec's credit tie
  rule and asks the operator to choose; the harness's fixed "Approved." reply does not choose. Three
  baseline and one candidate S2 SoMi draws ended below full green for this reason. S2 is distribution-only.
- One baseline SoMi draw (S1, seed 5) is invalid on `leak:other-draw`: a truncated copy of the draw's own
  directory in an audit-log line. The guard fails closed and was not loosened.
- Per-draw caps were $2 (SoMi) and $1 (bare), against $6 and $2 in the committed baseline; no draw reached its cap.
