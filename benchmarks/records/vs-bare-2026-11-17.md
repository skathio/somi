# somi5 against bare, small and medium, 2026-11-17

Plain SoMi against a bare session, with no staged workflow. 16 small and 10 medium registered tasks, 3 draws per task and arm. Session
model claude-sonnet-5-5, low tier claude-haiku-5-5, high tier claude-opus-5-5. Bare ran once; both SoMi versions are scored against
the same bare draws.

- **somi5 now:** `release/next` at 5bf4c52, with the size-triggered design review removed.
- **somi5 + boundary rules:** 35d794d, the same with three rules in the digest's security floor: take from a caller only the fields
  it may set, bound what untrusted input can cost, and read the diff as attacker and as operator before finishing a change at a trust
  boundary.

The earlier release records (`release-small-2026-11-16.md`, and the medium one in the bench) ran with the design review on, which
fired on 11 of 96 small somi5 draws. Their somi5 cost is superseded by this record. Their bare arms stay valid.

## What it says

- **On small work the rules did what they were for.** Reviewers ran as often (they start on what the diff touches), but found no
  Blocker or Major in 20 reviews against 7 before, so no draw needed a second review. Cost per solved task fell 21%, and solves rose
  by three, on tasks with a boundary (N10, H4, N17).
- **On medium work they changed little.** Solves are equal in all three arms; cost is 5% lower; strict successes and findings moved
  by one and two, inside the noise at 3 draws.
- **Against bare,** plain SoMi solves more small tasks (47 vs 36) and passes the strict standard far more often on both segments
  (35 vs 23, 16 vs 9), mostly because bare fails the quality gate on two thirds of medium draws. Medium hidden-test solves are equal.
- At 3 draws per task these are directions, not significant results.
